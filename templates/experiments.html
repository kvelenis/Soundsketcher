Research Context

The indefinite-pitch showcase is backed by the JASA Express Letters paper Beyond fundamental frequency: Pitch height judgments in Indefinite-Pitch sounds by Konstantinos Velenis, Asteris Zacharakis, and Emilios Cambouropoulos. The noise-tonal page presents study materials and exploratory analysis for a related SoundSketcher questionnaire workflow.
