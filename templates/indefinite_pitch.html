Publication Context

Beyond fundamental frequency: Pitch height judgments in Indefinite-Pitch sounds
Konstantinos Velenis, Asteris Zacharakis, and Emilios Cambouropoulos
JASA Express Letters, 2026. DOI: 10.1121/10.0043569

Study Snapshot

48 listeners
19 non-harmonic sounds
1 sinusoidal adjustment task

Methodology

  • Participants first used three training examples to learn the interface and set a comfortable listening level.
  • For each study sound, they adjusted a sine tone until it matched the perceived pitch height of the sound.
  • When no convincing pitch match was available, they could mark the trial as no match.

Core Findings

  • Listeners can make reliable pitch-height distinctions even when periodic cues are weak or absent.
  • A loudness-weighted spectral centroid is a useful predictor of perceived pitch height in these sounds.
  • Sounds with greater spectrotemporal variability tend to produce less accurate or more dispersed matches.

Why It Matters Here

SoundSketcher needs a vertical pitch-like mapping for electroacoustic and environmental sounds, not only for harmonic tones. This experiment supports a practical idea: when fundamental frequency is unreliable, perceptually weighted spectral information can still guide graphical placement.
